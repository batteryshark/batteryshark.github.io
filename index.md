These are old and current notes from reverse engineering, game hacking, compatibility work, hardware dongles, Windows internals, arcade boards, and the occasional "this should not work, but let's find out" engineering detour.

The archive is intentionally readable rather than glossy. Some claims are artifacts of their original date, but the useful parts are the method: how the target was explored, where the assumptions broke, and what the tooling revealed.
